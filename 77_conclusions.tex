\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
This paper introduced a simulation of online parameter estimation framework for rigid multi-quadrotor payload transportation, driven by a Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) estimator. By embedding Euler-Lagrange and Euler-Poincaré equations into the loss function, the network successfully converges on its estimates proofing that the network cannot rely solely by the data.

Simulation results validate that integrating this PINN-based estimator into the control allocation matrix enables the system to identify unmodeled payload mass and center-of-mass offsets. The approach effectively arrests transient lateral drift and maintains stable trajectory tracking within a single flight for all tested trajectories. Furthermore, the results indicate that even when absolute parameter convergence is incomplete, the resulting dynamic thrust reallocation is sufficient to restore the baseline controller's authority and counteract unmodeled disturbance torques.

Future work will extend this methodology to software-in-the-loop (SITL) environments by incorporating a publish-subscribe messaging architecture to connect the controller, the simulation, and the online estimator. Ultimately, validating this distributed architecture will serve as the foundational step to transition this framework from simulation to real-world flight testing.
