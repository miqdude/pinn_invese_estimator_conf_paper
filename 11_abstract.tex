\begin{abstract}
Cooperative multi-quadrotor systems rigidly attached to a common frame provide scalable solutions for heavy-lift transportation. However, unmodeled payload mass and unknown spatial offsets can introduce continuous disturbance torques, degrading controller performance and causing severe lateral drift. This paper introduces a fixed time-step simulation of Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) parameter estimation framework to dynamically identify unmodeled off-center payloads. To achieve online parameter adaptation, the proposed PINN utilizes continuous gradient descent over a short-term receding data horizon. By embedding translational and rotational physics residuals derived from Euler-Lagrange and Euler-Poincaré equations, the network enforces globally valid rigid-body constraints. The estimated parameters are then continuously fed into the system's control allocation matrix, enabling immediate and adaptive thrust reallocation. Simulations confirm that the integrated PINN estimator converges the desired parameters, arrests transient lateral drift, and successfully restores trajectory tracking stability during dynamic flight maneuvers. \footnote{The source code for the PINN estimator and simulation environment is available at: \url{https://github.com/miqdude/pinn_invese_estimator_conf_repo}}
\end{abstract}
