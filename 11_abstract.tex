\begin{abstract}
Cooperative multi-quadrotor systems rigidly attached to a common frame excel at heavy-lift transportation. However, unmodeled payload can introduce continuous disturbance torques, degrading controller performance and causing severe lateral drift. This paper introduces an in-the-loop Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) parameter estimation framework to dynamically identify unmodeled off-center payload. Unlike classical batch least-squares or Concurrent Learning frameworks that require persistent excitation or mathematically full-rank history stacks, the proposed PINN utilizes continuous gradient descent over a short-term receding horizon. By utilizing translational and rotational physics residuals using Euler-Lagrange and Euler-Poincaré equations directly on the Special Orthogonal group, $SO(3)$, the estimated parameters continuously update the control allocation matrix, enabling adaptive thrust reallocation. The Simulations confirm that the PINN estimator rapidly converges, arrests transient lateral drift, and successfully restores trajectory tracking stability. \footnote{The source code for the PINN estimator and simulation environment is available at: \url{https://github.com/miqdude/pinn_invese_estimator_conf_repo}}
\end{abstract}
