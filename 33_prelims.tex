\section{Preliminaries}

\subsection{Physics-Informed Parameter Estimation}
Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) embed known physical laws into the
deep learning optimization process. Unlike purely data-driven models, PINNs
treat the governing Ordinary or Partial Differential Equations as explicit
regularizers within the loss function \cite{raissi2019physics,mojgani_kolmogorov_2023}.

The network consists of fully connected layers $\mathcal{N}$ parameterized by
weights and biases $\theta$ \cite{raissi2019physics}. Consider a dynamical
system governed by $\mathcal{F}(u, \dot{u}, \ddot{u}, t) = 0$. The PINN acts
as a surrogate mapping time $t$ to a predicted state $\hat{u}(t)$:

\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{N}_{\theta}(t) = \hat{u}(t).
\end{equation}

\noindent Training minimizes a composite loss $\mathcal{L}_{total}$ balancing
empirical observation against physical consistency. The data loss anchors the
network to measurements via the Mean Squared Error (MSE) over $n$ time steps
$t_i$:

\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}_{data} = \lambda_1 \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n}
    \left\lVert \hat{u}(t_i) - u_{true}(t_i) \right\rVert^2 .
\end{equation}

Simultaneously, the network evaluates the physical validity of its predictions. Rather than relying on numerical approximation, PINNs leverage Automatic Differentiation (Autograd) to compute exact analytical derivatives of the output with respect to the input (e.g., $\dot{\hat{u}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{N}_{\theta}}{\partial t}$). These derivatives are substituted back into the governing equation to form a physics residual $R(t) = \mathcal{F}(\hat{u}, \dot{\hat{u}}, \ddot{\hat{u}}, t)$. The physics loss ($\mathcal{L}_{physics}$) penalizes any deviation across $n$ collocation points:

\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}_{physics} = \lambda_2 \frac{1}{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} \left\lVert R(t_j) \right\rVert^2 .
\end{equation}

The total loss is calculated as a sum of these objectives, $\mathcal{L}_{total} = \mathcal{L}_{data} + \mathcal{L}_{physics}$. By minimizing this composite function, the network learns to predict states that not only match the data but also strictly obey the underlying physical mechanics of the system \cite{mojgani_kolmogorov_2023}.
